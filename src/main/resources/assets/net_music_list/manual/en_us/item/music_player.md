# Portable Player

A player that allows you to carry and play songs anywhere, and it is the most complex item (in terms of mechanics) in the entire mod.

******

## Usage

Hold a music disc or playlist in your inventory, then **left-click** on the Portable Player to insert it, or **right-click** to take it out.

After inserting, the player will display the currently playing song's information. If playback starts successfully, a HUD showing the currently playing song will also appear in the top-left corner.

## Quick Song Selection

Right-click while holding the Portable Player or **hold down** the quick song selection key to open the song selection menu on the right side.

Use the scroll wheel to select, **Alt** to show translations, and **Shift** to scroll faster (configurable).

Click again or release the key to confirm your selection.

## HUD

When a **Portable Player** is playing a song **owned by you**, a song information HUD will appear in the top-left corner.

## Entity

Sneak right-click on the ground or throw the item to transform it into a Portable Player entity. Please refer to the relevant section for details.
