# Ender Player

A Music Player capable of playing music regardless of distance. (Subject to chunk loading limits.)

******

## Usage

Fundamentally similar to the vanilla Music Player, but allows adding specific players who can hear the sound.

**Sneak + Right-click with an empty hand** to add players. If no players are added, all players can hear it by default.
