# Net Music: Better Experience

This mod adds many new features to the Net Music (essentially implementing whatever features I dreamt up).

This mod is the most downloaded GLY mod, so I believe it deserves a dedicated tutorial.

The tutorial may not cover every possible issue. In such cases, you can inquire on the [MCMod homepage](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/20566.html) or [GitHub](https://github.com/gly091020/netMusicListNeoForge).

However, please try to avoid asking simple questions like "Where is the config file?".
