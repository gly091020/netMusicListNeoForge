# Iam Music Player Renewed

> The author has put most of their energy and ideas into Iam, but the mod's download count hasn't been great. Coupled with the pressure of academic studies, the author has somewhat lost enthusiasm for mod development. I have no choice but to advertise here; please forgive me...

Iam Music Player Renewed is a music player mod that rivals this mod in every detail and even surpasses it in quality.
(Only for 1.21.1)

It features basic playlist display, multiplayer playback, and NetEase Cloud support. Additionally, thanks to a different underlying structure, it fixes some long-standing issues with online music machines.

I believe Iam Music Player Renewed will surely provide you with a wonderful music playback experience!
