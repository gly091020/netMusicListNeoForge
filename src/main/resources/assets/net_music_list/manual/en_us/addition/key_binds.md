# Key Bindings

This mod has a total of four key bindings, which are:

-   **Accelerated Scrolling**

    Default Key: Left Shift

    Function: Increases the scrolling speed during quick song selection.

-   **Music Switching**

    Default Key: Unassigned

    Function: When holding a Portable Player in the inventory, press and hold this key to open the quick selection interface. Release the key to confirm the selection.

-   **Music Panic Stop**
    > _Essentially a "boss key"_

    Default Key: Unassigned

    Function: Mutes the music currently playing from the Portable Player, hides the top-left HUD, and displays "Music Panic Stop Active..." at the top of the screen.

-   **(Song Selection) Show Translation**

    Default Key: Left Alt

    Function: Displays the translation during quick song selection.
